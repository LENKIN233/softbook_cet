## Surface

Complete learner-facing Learning, Space, Statistics, Mine, auth, and membership design for iOS, Android, phone web, tablet, and desktop web.

## Accepted Baseline

No accepted mobile visual baseline existed. The run must materially replace the rejected `mvr-15` proof and the runnable but visually provisional React Native UI.

## Product Truth

Softbook is a CET4/6 single-card learning product. The system chooses the next card. Learning stays primary; Space visibly preserves 馆 → 组 → 盒 → 卡. The five interaction families remain distinct. Flip has exactly “有把握” = mint and “再回看” = amber; those self-assess states never enter auto-scored families.

## Hard Constraints

One focal task and one primary action per state; no horizontal overflow at 320/360/393/430; dedicated tablet and desktop composition; no internal prompt/spec/debug/reviewer copy on learner screens; no paper, glass, monochrome wireframe, dashboard, poster, target, or universal-pill direction; design-only acceptance must precede RN implementation.

## Soft Objectives

Consumer-app completion, CET trust, warm energy without childishness, low cognitive burden, immediately legible selection/result states, recognizable stable brand, and feasible RN/Web mapping.

## Source Artifacts

Product and interaction specs listed above, `docs/design/canon.md`, `docs/design/single-card-ux-contract.md`, existing interaction-motion artifacts, repository development fixture copy, and frozen rejected search-run evidence.

## Forbidden Drift

Generic word-study app, browse-first learning, monochrome outlines, lilac glass, paper/editorial styling, high-saturation poster blocks, architectural Space shell, counters as product core, hidden audio autoplay, four-level self-assessment, and red “再回看”.

## Candidate Budget

Nine first-generation directions; hard-filter five; compare four survivors in a connected three-edge graph; synthesize one targeted winner; require exact rendered proof, independent UI/UX/accessibility review, and delegated product-owner checkpoint before promotion.
