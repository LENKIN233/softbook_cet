## Product Truth

Both preserve one current task, canonical interaction meaning, truthful
feedback, and Space containment as `product_truth`. Their sheets, panes,
palette, and navigation remain `implementation_hypothesis`.

## Task Clarity

mvn-05 is stronger on phone because it owns the decision region. mvn-08 is
thinner on phone but separates task and context more plausibly on tablet.

## Space Or Interaction Fit

mvn-08 offers the better relative tablet responsibility split; mvn-05 stretches
its phone sheet into an oversized tablet control region. Neither proves all
libraries, complete recovery, formal audio, or native behavior.

## Visual System Fit

mvn-08 has the stronger structural seed but an enterprise-workspace tone,
purple-gray/system/brick color conflict, Unicode glyphs, and empty lower pane.
mvn-05 has better phone action hierarchy but weaker tablet composition.

## Implementation Mapping

Use mvn-08 only as a tablet relationship hypothesis and mvn-05 only as a phone
action-region hypothesis in a newly drawn synthesis. Neither maps directly to RN.

## Rationale

Cross-device structure wins this comparison. The result is a bracket edge, not
a shortlist or design selection.

## Borrowable Fragments

Combine mvn-08's tablet task/context allocation with mvn-05's phone action
ownership, then redraw palette, icons, ratios, motion, and recovery states.

## Rejected Fragments

Reject both exact palettes, glyph navigation, mvn-05's tablet scale, mvn-08's
enterprise-workspace tone, and any claim of native readiness.
