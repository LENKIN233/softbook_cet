## Product Truth

Both preserve one current task, canonical interaction meaning, truthful
feedback, and Space containment as `product_truth`. Their rail, header, palette,
and sheet systems remain rejected `implementation_hypothesis`.

## Task Clarity

mvn-04 gives the task more usable phone width. mvn-03 allocates persistent
attention to an address rail before the learner needs Space context.

## Space Or Interaction Fit

mvn-03 makes location explicit but reproduces the already-vetoed spine/rail
mental model. mvn-04 keeps interaction shapes more legible, yet neither proves
full-library paths, native recovery, or causal motion.

## Visual System Fit

mvn-04 is less structurally disqualified, but its peach-brown palette and
nested sheets still feel dated. Both retain generic icons and weak tablet finish.

## Implementation Mapping

Neither candidate may map into RN. The rail adds presentation semantics that
product truth does not require; the header/sheet stack lacks accepted authority.

## Rationale

Avoiding a vetoed visual premise wins this comparison. That is not a positive
endorsement of mvn-04 and does not produce a reusable shell.

## Borrowable Fragments

No visual fragment is borrowed. Current context must remain compact and
understandable, with newly derived geometry.

## Rejected Fragments

Reject the persistent rail/spine, peach-brown palette, nested sheets, Unicode
navigation glyphs, cosmetic platform skins, and both tablet arrangements.
