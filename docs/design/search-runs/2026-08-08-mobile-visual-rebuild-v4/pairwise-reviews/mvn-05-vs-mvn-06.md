- Candidate B: mvn-06

## Reviewer Role

Strict UI/UX comparison of the exact learner documents. A pairwise result is a
narrow relative judgement only; it does not pass the shared P1 advancement gate.

## Winner

not advance and is not a provisional leader.

## Product Truth

Both preserve system-sequenced Learning, the five interaction families,
truthful results, and Space containment as `product_truth`. Sheet and block
systems remain `implementation_hypothesis`.

## Task Clarity

mvn-05 makes the phone action region visibly belong to the current task.
mvn-06 splits the prompt and action into bordered modules that resemble an
administrative form.

## Space Or Interaction Fit

mvn-05 gives Flip and the auto-scored interactions a more reachable decision
region. mvn-06 exposes boundaries clearly but fragments the first read. Neither
proves every Space path or complete pending/error/retry behavior.

## Visual System Fit

mvn-05 is more usable on phone; mvn-06 is denser and more tool-like. Both have
unfinished tablets, generic glyphs, cosmetic platform differences, and no
accepted causal motion.

## Implementation Mapping

Only mvn-05's phone prompt/action relationship may be redrawn in a new
synthesis. Neither exact sheet nor block system authorizes implementation.

## Rationale

Phone action ownership beats equal-weight structured blocks. The shared P1
findings still reject both candidates.

## Borrowable Fragments

Retain the phone action-region relationship from mvn-05, not its exact palette,
sheet height, radius, or tablet scale.

## Rejected Fragments

Reject mvn-06's form-like blocks, mvn-05's oversized tablet action region,
Unicode navigation glyphs, and browser-only finish claims.
