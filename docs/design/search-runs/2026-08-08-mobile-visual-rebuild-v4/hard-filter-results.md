# Mobile Visual Rebuild V4 — Hard Filter Results

## Filter Scope

The eight exact learner documents were checked against `product_truth`: one
system-sequenced current task, five distinct interaction silhouettes, truthful
feedback and recovery, library → group → box → card containment, exactly two
flip judgements, learner/reviewer isolation, and four top-level destinations.
Browser reflow, minimum targets, and operability are measured evidence floors,
not visual acceptance. Text/non-text contrast remains a required floor, but this
run did not bind a complete final-composite contrast matrix. Palette, chrome,
iconography, motion, platform adaptation, and tablet composition remain
`implementation_hypothesis`.

## Rejected Candidates

No candidate is rejected for a remaining P0 product-truth violation in the
rebuilt exact cohort. This does **not** mean that any candidate advances: every
candidate is held below by the shared P1 advancement blockers.

## Product Truth Violations

The rebuilt documents close the earlier false-success, missing-next,
non-operable interaction, static Space, and broken Auth-history defects. Swipe
now has real drag behavior plus labeled button alternatives. Empty and incorrect
submissions receive explicit feedback, and committed states expose one strongest
next action. No candidate adds module selection, four-level assessment, a sixth
audio interaction family, or a flat two-box Space replacement.

Terminal interaction retesting also closed two former P2 findings: after flip
commit, both assessment buttons are disabled; in elimination, a correct but
previously unselected item is forced into strike-through and labeled `应划除`.

## Layout Or Proof Violations

Every candidate remains blocked by shared P1 gaps:

- iOS and Android still rely on weak or placeholder iconography and insufficiently
  different platform behavior;
- causal motion is not demonstrated as a reviewed interaction artifact;
- Auth and Space do not yet prove complete pending, error, retry, and recovery
  behavior across all operations;
- only a representative Space path is deep-linked; the full library hierarchy is
  not proven;
- tablet compositions remain browser-layout evidence below product-grade visual
  quality;
- `speechSynthesis` is a design-proof convenience, not an approved or packaged
  audio asset.

The exact browser cohort passed 80/80 320 × 844 initial-state frames with
browser text at 200% across 8 candidates × 2 platform hypotheses × 5 Learning
interactions. The reproducible URL, measured fields, thresholds, and result are
recorded under “Narrow 200 Final” in `browser-evidence.md`. Final terminal testing at
iOS- and Android-framed 390 × 844
also passed for all eight candidates: no horizontal overflow, primary CTAs were
not covered by navigation, active controls met the 44px iOS / 48px Android
floors, and multiple choice remained 2 × 2. In mvn-08's 1024 × 768 tablet Lock
state, the CTA stayed inside the first viewport at y=671–719 on iOS and
y=689–737 on Android.

Native safe-area, IME, VoiceOver/TalkBack, physical-device, real native 200%
text, reduced-motion, and formal audio-asset verification remain open. The
closed browser floor is not native-device acceptance.
