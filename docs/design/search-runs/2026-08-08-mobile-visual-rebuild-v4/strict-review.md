## Closed P0 Truthfulness And Operability Defects

- Eight candidates now have physically separate learner DOMs and independent
  browser history rather than one shared skinnable document.
- Flip reveals its answer before self-assessment, announces no premature
  positive outcome, exposes exactly `有把握` = confident/mint and `再回看` =
  review/amber, then provides a real next action.
- Multiple choice, lock, elimination, and swipe have operable submit/result/next
  loops. Swipe supports actual drag gestures plus labeled button alternatives.
- Empty and incorrect submissions receive explicit local feedback; committed
  states expose one strongest CTA instead of competing actions.
- Representative Space browse/favorite/sleep/wake/return and successive Auth
  phone/code/error/resend/edit/back paths are operable.
- Route focus, `aria-current`, disclosure semantics, live feedback, and tabular
  Statistics numerals are present in the browser proof.
- Exact browser checks passed 80/80 initial states at 320 × 844 with browser
  200% text; the method and fields are bound in
  `browser-evidence.md#narrow-200-final`, including horizontal overflow, active
  target floors, visible-route exclusivity, and clipped-control checks.
- Final terminal checks passed for all eight iOS- and Android-framed 390 × 844
  states: no horizontal overflow, primary CTA clear of navigation, 44px iOS /
  48px Android minimum active controls, and 2 × 2 multiple choice.
- The mvn-08 1024 × 768 tablet Lock CTA is inside the first viewport at
  y=671–719 on iOS and y=689–737 on Android.

These closures prove design-browser behavior only. They do not convert the
candidate visual systems into accepted product design.

## Shared P1 Advancement Blockers

1. Platform iconography and differentiation are below product quality. Several
   destinations still use generic glyphs, while iOS and Android differ mainly by
   treatment rather than sufficiently native composition and behavior.
2. Causal motion is not proven by an accepted motion artifact. State changes are
   functional, but they do not yet communicate spatial or interaction causality.
3. Auth and Space do not cover complete pending, error, retry, duplicate-action,
   and recovery behavior for every operation.
4. Space proves one representative library → group → box → card path, not full
   deep-link behavior across every library and return location.
5. Tablet layouts are responsive browser arrangements, not yet a coherent,
   product-grade iPadOS/Android tablet experience.
6. Browser `speechSynthesis` proves only that an audio control can respond. It is
   not an approved content asset, private-download proof, native playback proof,
   or production audio contract.
7. Native safe-area, IME, VoiceOver/TalkBack, physical-device, and real native
   200% text behavior remain unverified; the closed browser terminal checks do
   not substitute for native acceptance.

Any one of these P1 findings blocks advancement; they are not deferred RN polish.
