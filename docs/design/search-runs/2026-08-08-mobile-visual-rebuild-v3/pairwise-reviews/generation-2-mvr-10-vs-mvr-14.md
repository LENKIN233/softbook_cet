# Generation 2 — MVR 10 vs MVR 14

## Pair

- Candidate A: `mvr-10`
- Candidate B: `mvr-14`

## Reviewer Role

Independent visual-system and UX-task review recorded after real browser
rendering. The candidate author records the verdict but does not self-approve
either direction.

## Winner

`no winner`. Both candidates were rejected after comparison.

## Visual Evidence

- Candidate A: `candidate-proofs/mutation-candidate-proofs.html#mvr-10`
- Candidate B: `candidate-proofs/mutation-candidate-proofs.html#mvr-14`

Both anchors render Learning front, inline result/analysis, and Space at
393 x 852. They are direction-level evidence only.

## Product Truth

`mvr-10` preserves answer -> analysis -> continuation more faithfully.
`mvr-14` preserves the lock silhouette and hierarchy more visibly. Neither
direction creates a trustworthy, recognizably Softbook CET product across the
whole proof: one drifts toward craft metaphor, the other toward a campaign
poster and youthful activity surface.

## Task Clarity

`mvr-10` is clearer for its 2 x 2 task because prompt, options, result, and
analysis follow one predictable column. `mvr-14` makes lock rows legible but its
large header field and diagonal cuts consume useful space. Candidate A wins this
criterion narrowly; that local win is not a direction win.

## Space Or Interaction Fit

`mvr-14` has the stronger interaction and Space structure: the lock rows visibly
progress and the nested hierarchy can be understood without relying only on
labels. `mvr-10` returns to rectangles inside rectangles and makes the textile
pocket more important than the current object.

## Visual System Fit

Neither passes. `mvr-10` puts dense warp lines behind content and lets the craft
material define identity. `mvr-14` uses saturated poster fields and resembles
the offset-block family. Neither demonstrates a stable Softbook mark and rhythm
that remain recognizable when the current collection changes.

## Implementation Mapping

`mvr-10`'s option/result/analysis order maps cleanly to existing task regions.
`mvr-14`'s lock rows and nested hierarchy also map structurally. Both visual
skins would require implementation-time invention to handle long copy, all
interaction families, tablet composition, large type, and platform chrome;
therefore neither is implementation-ready.

## Rationale

Selecting either direction would trade one P1 failure for another. The useful
parts are orthogonal structural fragments: A supplies result sequencing; B
supplies lock progression and Space containment. The honest outcome is no
winner, fragment harvest, and a later generation with a new brand foundation.

## Borrowable Fragments

- From `mvr-10`: 2 x 2 options, preserved selection, registered result, inline
  analysis, and delayed `下一张`.
- From `mvr-14`: vertically progressive lock rows, settled result, inline
  explanation, and hierarchy reinforced by containment/elevation.

## Rejected Fragments

- Textile texture, serrated borders, and pocket-as-Space.
- Poster palette, diagonal shell, oversized signal fields, and flag navigation.
- Any claim that winning a local criterion authorizes either visual direction.
