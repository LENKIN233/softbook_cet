## Product Truth Fit

The direction makes Learning-to-Space continuity immediately legible and renders physical hierarchy strongly. Its main product risk is that route and place may become more interesting than the CET question, causing Space browsing to leak into the primary learning path.

## Focal Object

A large folded map panel whose active stop contains the current question.

## First-Read Path

Current-stop question -> route-end reveal tab -> fold address -> legend navigation.

## Interaction Silhouette

Flip remains one large fold occupying most of the screen. The back opens the next fold to reveal answer and analysis, followed by exactly two assessment stamps.

## Spatial Model

Space zooms out from the current fold: library territory -> group route -> box block -> current card stop. Favorite is a pin mark, while sleep is an off-route rest pocket with a visible return route.

## State Language

Indigo route ink denotes the current library. Favorite is a small pin symbol attached to the stop. Sleep moves the stop into a desaturated side pocket; wake reconnects it to the route.

## Motion Causality

Reveal unfolds the next paper panel along a visible crease. Entering Space zooms out along the same route. Reduced motion replaces unfolding with a direct change of scale and content.

## Platform Strategy

Phone shows one fold and a compact map legend. Tablet can show current fold and parent territory together. PC web can expose more of the map, but cannot make free exploration the default study mode.

## Implementation Mapping

The current fold maps to Learning content, the route-end tab to the primary action, and the fold address to Space continuity. The territory, route, box block, current stop, favorite pin, and rest pocket map to existing Space semantics.

## Known Risks

Map language may overwhelm exam authority, the route can resemble a progress timeline, and zoom behavior may become decorative. The horizontal fold pattern must not encourage carousel browsing or hide the next action.

## Hard Filter Disposition

Rejected in generation 1. The route metaphor becomes more salient than the CET task, risks reviving timeline management, and encourages map browsing before system-sequenced learning. Borrowable fragment only: preserve the conceptual zoom from current card to parent Space when a later system finds a quieter visual form.
