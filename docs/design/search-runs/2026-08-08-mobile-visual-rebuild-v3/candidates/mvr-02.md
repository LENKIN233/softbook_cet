## Product Truth Fit

The question stays primary and the answer remains the reverse state of the same sheet. The visual authority resembles annotated exam material rather than an entertainment product. The risk is substantial: the back can read like a report and the sectional navigation can compete with the task. It is rendered to test that boundary, not to claim authority.

## Focal Object

A full-bleed ruled question sheet with one heavy indigo annotation margin.

## First-Read Path

Oversized question -> underlined instruction -> reveal footer -> sectional index.

## Interaction Silhouette

Flip uses a single sheet filling most of the viewport. The back keeps the sheet position and replaces the question field with answer, reasoning, and the two light self-assess actions.

## Spatial Model

Space is drawn as an archive register: library masthead -> grouped shelf bands -> numbered drawer -> clipped current card. It preserves hierarchy but is deliberately flat enough to test whether editorial authority can coexist with physical objecthood.

## State Language

Indigo rules identify the current library. Favorite uses a marginal star mark, while sleep uses a hatched drawer zone with a clear wake label. Self-assess colors remain small dots beside the two decisions.

## Motion Causality

The sheet pivots on its left rule during reveal. Space expands from the printed address line into the archive register. Reduced motion swaps front and back while keeping the same ruled frame.

## Platform Strategy

Phone uses a vertical broadsheet crop. Tablet can introduce two columns for question and analysis. PC web can use a wide examination desk, but must keep one question as the sole focal object.

## Implementation Mapping

Question, support, context, and analysis map to one persistent sheet region. Section tabs map only to top-level navigation. Space bands map to the accepted hierarchy and operations without creating filters or a reporting system.

## Known Risks

The composition risks reviving the previously rejected editorial direction, making the back feel like a report, and reducing Space to a diagram. Long Chinese text may become dense because the design relies on ruled alignment rather than separate surfaces.

## Hard Filter Disposition

Rejected in generation 1. The rendered back reads as a report, the top sectional index weakens thumb reach, and the archive register lacks convincing physical depth. Borrowable fragment only: the question and answer can retain a shared alignment grid; the broadsheet brand system is not reusable.
