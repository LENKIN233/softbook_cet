## Aesthetic Thesis

Soft, concentric pressure fields can make sound comprehension feel focused and embodied without becoming a music app, waveform dashboard, or technical instrument. Warm matte surfaces and off-center chambers create depth with no blur or glass.

## Focal Object

One large off-center sound chamber whose quiet center contains the `however` prompt and supporting cue.

## First-Read Path

## Motion Causality

Reveal compresses the outer band once and settles the analysis ring; entering Space pulls back from disc to chamber, channel, and hall. Reduced motion directly exposes the ring and retains the same focus transition. No decorative waveform loops, and audio never starts automatically.

## Platform Strategy

Phone uses one vertically cropped chamber and a low reveal mouth. Tablet portrait reveals the current chamber above its parent channel; landscape places the chamber beside its analysis ring within one broad hall, with bounded text measure. Android and iOS use their own back and audio focus conventions.

## Implementation Mapping

The quiet center maps to current prompt, reveal mouth to primary action, analysis ring to inline feedback, and frequency mark to address continuity. Hall, channel, chamber, disc, favorite notch, and damped sleep chamber map to existing Space hierarchy and operations.

## Known Risks
