## Failure Signal

Independent concept review found that Generation 1 did not contain eight
materially different visual systems. `mvr-01`, `mvr-02`, `mvr-05`, `mvr-06`,
`mvr-07`, and `mvr-08` shared too much of one family: a pale physical sheet,
one indigo edge or rule, a lower reveal control, a reverse state on the same
object, and a container-based Space. Naming the objects manual, broadsheet,
map, type case, tracing layer, or plan did not create six systems. The common
family also failed to separate a Softbook brand role from the current-collection
identity role.

The same review found direction-specific blockers: `mvr-02` revived the vetoed
editorial grammar, `mvr-04` revived a dark technical console, `mvr-06` pushed
toward toy-like controls, and `mvr-08` risked exposing engineering coordinates
instead of a learner-facing place.

Generation 1 is not discarded or renamed. Its failures stay inspectable so a
later comparison cannot pretend that a palette change solved population

## Expected Improvement

The mutation should produce at least eight directions that independent reviewers
perceive as structurally different when color and labels are removed. It should
also reveal whether a recognizable Softbook identity can come from rhythm,
object causality, and spatial behavior rather than from one recurring material
skin.

## Risk

Escaping the sheet family may create new drift: loud youth branding, novelty
controls, decorative metaphors, weak long-copy tolerance, or a Space diagram
that overpowers the study task. A high candidate count is not success if those
directions fail product truth, containment, contrast, or low operation cost.
