## Candidate ID

`cpl-06`

## Provenance

- Tool or model: Codex design search.
- Prompt: Compress the fifth-card boundary into one attached receipt with exact destinations and clear action priority.
- Source context pack: `docs/design/search-runs/2026-08-01-controlled-pilot-lifecycle/context-pack.md`
- Artifact: `docs/design/search-runs/2026-08-01-controlled-pilot-lifecycle/candidate-proofs/survivor-comparison.html#cpl-06`
- Screenshots: candidate-bound rendered evidence at the artifact anchor.

## Product Truth Fit

Passes the exact three-destination requirement and keeps continue dominant. The receipt metaphor must avoid billing language.

## Focal Object

The “这一轮已放好” receipt.

## First-Read Path

Completion statement -> quiet review/Space links -> continue.

## Interaction Silhouette

Resolve -> attached summary -> continue.

## Spatial Model

One compact location line preserves the card’s Space address.

## State Language

No score, percentage, ring, or celebration; only confirmed placement.

## Motion Causality

Receipt unfolds after the fifth confirmation; reduced motion shows it without transition.

## Platform Strategy

Phone-only and highly compatible with small screens and enlarged text.

## Implementation Mapping

Maps to a future round receipt with two quiet links and one primary button.

## Known Risks

Receipt styling can feel commercial if it uses totals, currency, perforation, or checkout language.

## Design Review Checklist Answers

Q1: One active-library accent.

Q2: Completion receipt first, continue last and strongest.

Q3: Compact address preserves Space continuity.

Q4: No payment or reward semantics.

Q5: Contained at 393 x 852.

Q6: No self-assess change.
