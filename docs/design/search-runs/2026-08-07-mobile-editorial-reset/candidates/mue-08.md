# Compact Card With Ambient Field

## Candidate ID

mue-08

## Provenance

- Tool or model: Codex design synthesis
- Prompt: Keep a compact top card and use a large calm color field below it to create breathing room.
- Source context pack: `context-pack.md`
- Artifact: No-render rationale: this recreates the exact shipped composition rejected by the product owner.
- Screenshots: No-render rationale: existing iOS and Android evidence already proves the dead-space failure.

## Product Truth Fit

The correct card exists, but its visual weight is too low to express the product's core task.

## Focal Object

The ambient field becomes larger than the current card.

## First-Read Path

Small card → unused field → oversized navigation, breaking task continuity.

## Interaction Silhouette

Fails the large current-card silhouette and makes interaction types look alike.

## Spatial Model

Space address becomes a small line with no physical relationship to the card.

## State Language

State relies on accent recoloring rather than object transformation.

## Motion Causality

Ambient motion has no learning-state cause.

## Platform Strategy

The empty field grows worse on tall phones and tablets.

## Implementation Mapping

It matches the existing implementation too closely and therefore cannot fix the hierarchy.

## Known Risks

Perceived incompleteness, generic template appearance, and weak primary action.

## Design Review Checklist Answers

Q1: Accent may be correct but lacks sufficient meaningful surface.

Q2: The current card is not the strongest object.

Q3: The canonical interaction silhouette fails.

Q4: No literal blocked token is needed; the composition itself is rejected.

Q5: There may be no overflow, but there is severe under-use of the viewport.

Q6: The flip control can exist, yet the overall Learning surface remains unqualified.
