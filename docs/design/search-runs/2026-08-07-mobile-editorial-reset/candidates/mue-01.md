## Product Truth Fit

Keeps one system-selected CET card as the task and treats color as stable library identity rather than decoration.

## Focal Object

A tall warm-paper card with a coral study margin rail.

## First-Read Path

Route progress → question → exam insight → attached flip edge → navigation.

## Interaction Silhouette

Large centered flip card with the action inside its lower edge; the back adds exactly two self-assess zones.

## Spatial Model

The margin carries the library/group identity and the address line reveals box/card position without opening module choice.

## State Language

Front is inquiry, back is explanation, mint is 有把握, amber is 再回看, and favorite remains a secondary text tool.

## Motion Causality

The paper object turns around its vertical center; reduced motion crossfades content while retaining the rail and address.

## Platform Strategy

Phone uses a vertical rail; large type converts it to a horizontal band. Tablet promotes the rail to a persistent gutter.

## Implementation Mapping

Maps to `LearningSurface` card container, action edge, tools, result state, and the shell's quiet navigation.

## Known Risks

The rail needs an accessibility fallback, and paper texture must remain subtle enough not to affect text contrast.
