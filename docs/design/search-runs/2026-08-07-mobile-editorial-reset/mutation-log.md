# Mutation Log

## Failure Signal

The accepted v1 reset and shipped screenshot look like a generic AI-generated glass dashboard: the card is too short, the background is visually over-invested, the bottom nav is oversized, and the primary action is detached from the study object.

## Targeted Mutation

Replace glass-panel grammar with one tall editorial paper object; move library identity to a vertical margin rail; attach the flip action to the card edge; reduce navigation to a quiet text rail; express Space as nested physical compartments rather than rounded list items.

## Expected Improvement

The first-read path becomes CET question → exam insight → action, the current card becomes unmistakable when blurred, and the product gains a recognizable “exam margin note” identity instead of relying on fashionable material effects.

## Risk

Paper material could become nostalgic or notebook-like, and a vertical rail could reduce usable width under accessibility type. The RN implementation must switch the rail to a horizontal header at large font scales.

## Result

The mutation improved focal scale, action causality, product distinctiveness, and Space continuity in the rendered 393 × 852 proof. It remains design-only pending product-owner acceptance and platform expansion.
