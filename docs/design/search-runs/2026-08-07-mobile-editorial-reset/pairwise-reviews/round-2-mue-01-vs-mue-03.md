# Pairwise Review: mue-01 vs mue-03

## Pair

- Candidate A: `mue-01`
- Candidate B: `mue-03`

## Reviewer Role

UI/UX director review for emotional tone, task clarity, library identity, interaction specificity, accessibility, and cross-platform mapping.

## Winner

Candidate A, `mue-01`.

## Visual Evidence

- Candidate A: `candidate-proofs/mobile-editorial-candidates.html#mue-01`
- Candidate B: `candidate-proofs/mobile-editorial-candidates.html#mue-03`

## Product Truth

Both keep Learning primary. `mue-01` better supports calm, low-burden study; `mue-03` feels more like an answer console and less like a physical knowledge card.

## Task Clarity

`mue-03` is fastest for auto-scored options. `mue-01` is clearer across front, back, hint, and Space continuity because all states remain one object.

## Space Or Interaction Fit

`mue-01` has a stronger flip identity. `mue-03`'s answer rows can be harvested for lock or elimination but do not define the whole product.

## Visual System Fit

`mue-01` avoids returning to a dark, near-monochrome theme. `mue-03` uses color well but the dark-plum surround becomes visually heavy across long sessions.

## Implementation Mapping

Both are feasible. `mue-01` needs a large-text rail fallback; `mue-03` needs separate adaptations for every interaction to avoid shared-row sameness.

## Rationale

Choose `mue-01` as the app identity and borrow `mue-03`'s compact answer rhythm only inside auto-scored card bodies.

## Borrowable Fragments

Compact single-line answer rows, tabular option labels, and a restrained selected-state fill from `mue-03`.

## Rejected Fragments

Reject the full dark-plum page environment and any design that makes all five interactions share the same answer-row silhouette.
